## Selected work

### Two digital transformations (Lexipol, 2020 to 2026)

I identified a systemic misalignment between legacy technology and evolving
business models, built the business case, secured executive investment, and
led two related digital transformations from business case through adoption.

- **Lead-to-cash rebuild.** As project owner, I led the replacement of
  Salesforce and Airtable with a new order management and billing platform
  integrated with NetSuite. I partnered with Finance and technical contractors,
  mitigated cutover data quality risks across CRM and audience systems, and
  partnered with engineering and technical architects on target-state
  architecture, aligning technology with redesigned processes and embedding
  data governance.
- **Martech transformation.** I led the selection process, selected vendors,
  and negotiated contracts, unifying customer data, CRM, and audience systems.
- I also transformed performance metrics and reporting for internal teams and
  customers, changing how results were measured and shared.
- I coordinated workstreams across Product, Engineering, Marketing, Sales,
  Finance, and Operations, managing dependencies and sequencing, and set up
  tracking and reporting, with recurring reviews and decision tracking, for
  cross-cutting initiatives directed by senior leadership.

- Ask me about: business cases, process redesign, target-state design,
  vendor selection, and cutover risk.

### Post-acquisition integration (Lexipol, 2020 to 2026)

- Ask me about: acquisition integration and integration playbooks.

### Product Operations, built from scratch (Lexipol, 2020 to 2026)

I built the Product Operations function from scratch, implemented Aha! for

- Ask me about: standing up an operations function, roadmap and capacity

### AI-assisted monthly reporting (Lexipol)

I built a semi-automated, AI-assisted monthly reporting workflow that reduced
manual preparation. AI gathers and organizes the data, and a person reviews
each stage before distribution.

### Mayron Studio operations app (my own resale business, 2026)

I run Mayron Studio, a resale business selling estate-sale and vintage items
on Etsy, eBay, and Depop. I mapped the business as one end-to-end process and
built a single, fully functional web app to run it, replacing a set of
separate, disconnected workflows. I designed the process and the app, and
built it with Claude.

- The workflow runs through six stages that share one set of data: Source,
  Receive, Active, Sold, Finance, and Reporting.
- It works alongside Nifty, which handles marketplace posting and listing
  management, rather than replacing it. I connected Nifty to Claude in
  October 2026.
- It runs on both iPhone and computer, and I keep improving it as I use it.

- Ask me about: end-to-end process design, building operational tools with
  AI, and integrating with existing systems instead of replacing them.
